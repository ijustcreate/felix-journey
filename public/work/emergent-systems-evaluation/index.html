Evaluation problem

Complex behavior invites premature mythology.

A particle cloud can appear to hunt, protect, reproduce or collaborate. An AI output can appear to reason, understand or intend. In both cases, visual or linguistic plausibility is evidence of an output—not automatic evidence of the story we tell about it.

Emergent Complexity keeps a deliberate gap between persistent measurable patterns and claims about agency. “Entity” means a tracked simulation structure. Classification is heuristic. Uncertainty reduces confidence in irreversible interventions. Seeded experiments, checkpoints and replay checksums make surprising behavior inspectable rather than mystical.

Observation

A cohesive pattern persisted for a measurable duration.

Interpretation

The pattern may exhibit behavior consistent with an agent candidate.

Causal test

Intervene, replay and compare against a controlled baseline.

Claim boundary

Report confidence and alternatives; do not upgrade metaphor into fact.

Evaluation lifecycle

Move from particles to claims one gate at a time.

01

Detect structure

Density, cohesion, persistence and stability establish whether a pattern is measurable.

02

Track through time

Identity, trajectory and change distinguish a persistent structure from a transient visual accident.

03

Classify with uncertainty

Behavioral labels remain heuristic and expose confidence rather than impersonating scientific certainty.

04

Intervene reversibly

Compare controlled changes, preserve checkpoints and reduce confidence when the intervention destroys future possibilities.

05

Reproduce before generalizing

Replay exact state, compare checksums, vary seeds and separate one compelling run from a robust finding.

Transfer to AI evaluation

The same intellectual hygiene applies to model behavior.

Simulation questionAI evaluation analogue
Can the state be replayed?Can the prompt, model/version, tools, context and trajectory be reconstructed?
Did a persistent structure form?Does the capability recur across paraphrases, seeds and challenge sets?
Is the label observational or interpretive?Are we measuring task success or inferring reasoning/understanding from fluent output?
What changed after intervention?Does removing context, changing tools or perturbing instructions reveal the causal dependency?
What is the cost of being wrong?Should uncertainty reduce autonomy, require verification or block an irreversible action?
A compelling pattern can deserve investigation without deserving worship.Causal-humility principle

Repository evidence

Verification exists as an executable contract.

The repository includes a deterministic reference engine, checkpoint download/restore, seeded runs and a verification script that executes 10,000 particles for 1,000 steps twice and compares the complete result. CPU, 2D GPU and 3D GPU surfaces share the same evaluation concepts, while detector coverage and hybrid-life work remain explicitly experimental.
