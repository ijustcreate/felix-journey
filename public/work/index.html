Operating method

Quality is not a department at the end of the hallway.

It is the structure that lets a team understand what happened, reproduce it, choose the right boundary, and verify that the cure did not murder the patient.

01

Observe without inflating

Separate what the system produced from what we infer about intelligence, intent, quality or causality.

02

Collapse the possibility space

Vary one meaningful condition at a time, preserve evidence, and reduce a vague failure to an engineering-ready mechanism.

03

Protect the human consequence

Measure whether state, permissions, feedback and recovery support the person—not merely whether the code reached a success branch.
